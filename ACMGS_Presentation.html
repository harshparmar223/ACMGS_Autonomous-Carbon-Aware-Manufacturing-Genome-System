ACMGS

Autonomous Carbon-Aware Manufacturing Genome System

Making every manufacturing decision smarter, more efficient, and lower-carbon.

AI + IoT + Optimization2026
01 / Problem Statement

Manufacturers can see energy use, but cannot act on it intelligently.

  • Energy costs and carbon-compliance pressure continue to rise.
  • Production schedules rarely adapt to changing grid-carbon intensity.
  • Machine degradation is often discovered only after quality, efficiency, or uptime suffers.
  • Critical data remains fragmented across sensors, MES/ERP systems, and manual records.

The gap: Conventional tools monitor consumption. They do not recommend the best operational decision in real time.

ACMGS02
03 / Innovation & Uniqueness

One integrated system, not another dashboard.

Core innovations

  • Energy DNA: LSTM-based machine energy signatures detect anomalous and degrading behaviour.
  • Batch Genome: A unified representation of material, process, energy, and carbon variables.
  • Multi-objective optimization: Balances yield, quality, energy consumption, and CO2 emissions.

Operational advantage

  • Carbon-aware scheduling: Aligns flexible production with lower-carbon grid periods.
  • Dynamic recommendations: Uses trends, thermal conditions, maintenance indicators, and confidence scores.
  • Open integration: Connects real-time devices, existing industrial systems, and reporting workflows.

Unique value: ACMGS turns manufacturing data into carbon-aware operational decisions, not just reports.
